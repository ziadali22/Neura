## 📸 How to Scan a Document

### Method 1: Quick Scan (Recommended)

1. **Tap the blue camera icon** in the top-right corner of any folder card
2. **Scan your document** - VisionKit will auto-detect and capture
3. **Add more pages** or tap "Save" when done
4. **Name your document** - default name is pre-filled, edit if needed
5. **Tap "Save Document"** - Done! ✨

**⏱ Total time: ~10 seconds**

## 🎯 Quick Tips

### Scanning Tips
- ✅ Good lighting improves scan quality
- ✅ Hold phone steady and parallel to document
- ✅ VisionKit auto-detects edges - no need to be perfect
- ✅ Scan multiple pages for multi-page documents

### Naming Tips
- ✅ Default names include category and date
- ✅ Keep names descriptive: "Annual Physical - Blood Work"
- ✅ Names help you find documents later
- ✅ Can't save with empty name (button is disabled)

### Organization Tips
- ✅ Use appropriate folder (Blood Tests, Prescriptions, etc.)
- ✅ Documents are grouped by month in folder view
- ✅ Newest documents appear at top
- ✅ Folder count shows total documents

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Camera doesn't open?
- Check camera permission in Settings → Neura
- Allow camera access

### Scanner shows wrong category?
- Make sure you tapped the correct folder's camera button
- Category is shown in naming screen: "Saving to [Category]"

### Can't save document?
- Document name cannot be empty
- Enter at least one character

### Document not appearing?
- Check the correct folder (tap folder card to view)
- Documents grouped by month - scroll to current month
